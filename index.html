About the property

Description property

Situated in the townland of Altavelid, within the village of Ballintogher on C 0.2 of an acre site, this modern four-bedroom detached home offers an ideal opportunity for families seeking space, comfort and countryside living.

The property has been well maintained throughout and is ready for immediate occupation. Designed with modern family living in mind, the home provides bright, spacious accommodation with a practical layout.

To the rear, a paved patio area overlooks a generous private garden, creating the perfect space for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply relaxing while enjoying the surrounding countryside.

Located within easy reach of local amenities, schools and sporting facilities, this attractive home combines the tranquillity of village life with convenient access to Sligo Town and the surrounding area.
